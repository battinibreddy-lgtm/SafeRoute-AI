# User Manual

## Route Analysis

1. Enter the starting location.
2. Enter the destination location.
3. Click "Find Safest Route".
4. Review:

   * Risk Score
   * Route Status
   * Suggested Route

## Map Features

* View accident blackspots.
* Click on map locations for AI risk analysis.
* View route line between source and destination.
* View start and destination markers.

## Risk Levels

* Low Risk (Safe)
* Medium Risk (Moderate)
* High Risk (Dangerous)
